## Platform

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, hosted on GitHub Pages with the dikenbros.com domain pointed from GoDaddy. Confirmed by Jad on 2 Sep 2026.

## Users

Primary: decision-makers at brands and partners weighing Diken as a partner. Delivery platforms (Talabat, Careem, HungerStation, noon), quick-service restaurant groups, vehicle and parts principals (Hero, SYM, Motul, Wabco, BPW), banks and financiers (Etihad Bank, Commercial Bank, Microfund for Women), and public bodies. They arrive from a referral, a meeting, or a search, usually on a phone, and need to know within seconds what Diken is, how big it is, and how to reach it. Confirmed 5 Sep 2026.

Secondary (not the hero's target): people who want to become captains, and press. Served by the Delivery and Impact pages.

## Product Purpose

Corporate site for Diken Bros, a Jordanian family group founded in 1990. It replaces a broken Google Site. Success: a partner-side visitor understands the group, trusts its scale and record, and contacts the company.

## Positioning

Diken is the company of firsts in Jordan's delivery economy: first to onboard captains with disabilities, first women captains, first zero-down zero-interest financing for people with no vehicle and no job, first electric swap scooter in Jordan, and an operation run with AI agents. That record sits on a 35-year heritage of spare-parts distribution, exclusive agencies, and investments. A rival delivery fleet cannot truthfully claim the firsts; a rival distributor cannot claim the fleet.

## Operating Context

Four divisions. Delivery & Logistics: 5,000+ captains serving Talabat, Careem, HungerStation and noon across Amman, Riyadh and Dubai, 20,000+ orders a day, JOD 30M+ cash collected yearly, 80 company-owned vehicles. Distribution & Agencies: exclusive Jordan agent for Motul, Wabco and BPW, on spare-parts supply to multinationals since 1990. Motorcycles: Hero and SYM agents in Jordan. Investments: 50% of Samhouri & Diken (insulation and duct accessories; Izocam, SABIC Steel, Vents; projects at Le Royal, Sheraton Amman, City Mall, Queen Alia International Airport) and 25% of Areena (green scooter solutions, Blue Sharks exclusive agent, first battery-swap grid with Talabat and Total).

Technology: an AI agent for operations and cash collection; an agent integrated with Careem coordinating orders for leading quick-service restaurant brands, with a verified chat agent and a voice agent that escalates to humans; AI-generated social content and ad analysis; AI-assisted finance and sales reporting.

Vocabulary: "captains" for riders. "Diken" in headlines, "Diken Bros" in legal and footer.
