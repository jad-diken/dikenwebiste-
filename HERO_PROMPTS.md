## Consistency checklist before you send the photos
- Same grey background in every shot, no floor line.
- Full body, wheels included, nothing cropped.
- Same camera height and angle (chest height, three-quarter, facing camera-left).
- Same key light from camera-left. If one rider is lit from the right, the composite will look wrong.
- Riders 2–5 look ahead, only the Diken rider looks at camera.
- No logos or words on partner riders. Colour only. Real logos go in the strip under the hero.
- Generate 2–3 variants of each and keep the one whose bike angle matches the Diken rider's best.
