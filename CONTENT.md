# Diken Bros — site structure v3 (5 Sep 2026)

## The idea that holds it together
Diken is the company of firsts in Jordan's delivery economy: first with women captains, first with captains with disabilities, first with zero-down financing, first electric swap scooter, and now an AI-run operation. The heritage (1990, agencies, motorcycles, investments) is the foundation that makes the firsts credible. So the site reads: **Who we are → What we run → What we did first → How we run it → Who we work with.**

Vocabulary: "captains" for riders, site-wide. "Diken" alone in headlines, "Diken Bros" in legal/footer.
